Privacy

Privacy policy

What information I collect through this website, my phone line and sale alerts, and how I use it. The short version: I use it to help you, and I never sell it.

Email a question

Last updated November 11, 2026

Better Selling With Bailey ("I", "me" or "my") is an estate sale business in Erie County, Pennsylvania, owned and run by Dorian Bailey. This policy covers bettersellingwithbailey.com, my business phone line, and my email and text sale alerts.

What I collect

  • What you send me through a form. When you book a walkthrough, sign up for sale alerts, send a referral, refer a client, request a checklist or apply for a job, I receive what you type in: usually your name, phone number and email, plus details like your town, your situation, your timeline, what you're hunting for or your availability.
  • Calls and texts. When you call or text my business line, I receive your phone number and what you tell me. Calls may be answered by an automated assistant that takes a message, and calls may be recorded or transcribed so I can follow up accurately.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, the companies that host this site and serve its fonts receive standard information such as your IP address and browser type when a page loads. I don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, schedule a walkthrough and run your sale.
  • To send the sale alerts you signed up for.
  • To follow up on referrals and pay referral rewards.
  • To review job applications.
  • To keep the business records I'm required to keep, such as contracts and payment records.

Who I share it with

I don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. I share it only with the services that help me run the business, and only so they can do that job for me: the form service that delivers website messages to my email, my email provider, the service that answers my phone line, and the services that send my text and email alerts. I may also share information if the law requires it.

Text messaging: I do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or your text message opt-in and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected for sale alerts are used only to send those alerts.

Sale-alert texts

If you add your mobile number to a sale-alert signup, you agree to receive recurring text messages from Better Selling With Bailey about upcoming sales: the dates, the address and preview photos. Expect one or two messages a month; it can vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or HELP for help. Signing up is never required to shop a sale or to hire me. More detail is in the Terms.

Email

Sale-alert emails are only about sales. Reply and ask to be removed, or use the unsubscribe link if there is one, and I'll take you off the list.

How long I keep it

I keep information only as long as I need it for the reasons above. Contracts and payment records are kept as long as the law and tax rules require.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the information I have about you, or to stop contacting you. Email bettersellingwithbailey@gmail.com or call (814) 527-1079. I'll take care of it, except for records I'm required to keep.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, and I limit who can see it to me and the services listed above. No method of sending or storing information online is perfectly secure.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
