Small-town homes with outbuildings

Around Girard, Lake City and Platea, a sale often includes more than the house: a detached garage, a pole barn or a workshop full of tools and equipment. I handle all of it, from pricing to the last item out the door, and you don't need to be there.

Getting buyers to make the drive

Western Erie County is a drive for some shoppers, so advertising matters more out here. Photos and descriptions go out online and to my sale-alert list before the sale, so collectors and serious buyers decide to make the trip from Erie and beyond.

Moving a parent into senior living?

Nearby options include Pleasant Ridge Manor on West Ridge Road and Battles Village on Main Street in Girard. If a move-in date is already set, I'll plan the sale backward from it so the house is cleared in time.
