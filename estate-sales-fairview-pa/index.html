Bigger lots, more to sell

Fairview homes often sit on bigger lots than in town, with a garage, pole barn or workshop on top of everything in the house. Tools, shop equipment, lawn and garden gear and outdoor equipment have steady local demand, so I price and sell all of it, inside and out, then clear what's left.

Bringing buyers to the west side

Fairview is on the west side of the county, between Millcreek and Girard, with Route 5, Route 20 and I-90 all running through it. Every sale is advertised online and to my sale-alert list, so collectors and serious buyers from across the county make the trip, not just the neighbors.

Moving a parent into senior living?

Nearby communities include Fairview Manor on Manchester Road and Manchester Commons on West Lake Road. If a move-in date is already set, I'll plan the sale backward from it so the house is cleared in time.

Fairview questions

Can you sell what's in the garage or barn too?

Yes. Tools, equipment and outdoor gear often sell well, and they can be set up in the garage or barn while the rest of the sale runs inside.

Will buyers come out to Fairview?

Yes. Fairview is minutes from Millcreek and I-90, and every sale is advertised online and to my sale-alert list, so buyers know what's there before they drive out.

Can the whole sale be held in the garage?

Yes. A sale can run inside the house, in the garage or barn, or in the yard, whichever works best for the property and the season. The fee is the same.
