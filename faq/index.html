Cost and getting paid

How much does it cost?

A flat $995 fee plus 25% of total sales. Pricing, staging, advertising, running the sale and removing leftovers are all included, and the walkthrough before you book is free. The one optional extra is $295 to haul away large furniture. See what estate sales cost in Erie. More: What estate sales cost in Erie

When do I pay?

A $500 deposit when you book holds your sale dates, and it counts toward the $995. Everything else, the rest of the fee and the 25%, comes out of the sale proceeds, so there's no second check to write.

When do I get paid?

The day the sale ends, with a written statement of what the sale brought in and what was deducted. More: How you get paid from an estate sale

How much would I keep from a $10,000 estate sale?

With my pricing, the total cost is $3,495. You've already paid $500, so $2,995 comes out of the proceeds and you're paid $7,005 the day the sale ends. More: How you get paid after an estate sale, line by line

How is the estate sale company's fee paid?

With me, a $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the flat $995 fee. The rest of the fee and the 25% of sales come out of the proceeds, so there's no second check to write. More: How you get paid after an estate sale, line by line

Is the cleanout extra?

No. Removing leftovers is included with every sale. The only extra is the optional $295 for hauling away large furniture. More: Estate cleanouts in Erie County

What is a normal estate sale commission in Erie?

HomeLight reports that Erie estate sale companies typically charge 35% to 50% of total sales, with minimums of $5,000 to $10,000 in expected sales. More: What estate sale companies in Erie charge, and what you actually keep

Is a lower commission always better?

Not always. Compare what you'd keep after every fee, and look at the company's reviews, insurance, contract and how fast they pay. More: What estate sale companies in Erie charge, and what you actually keep

Hiring an estate sale company

What does an estate sale company do?

It sells the contents of a house for you: it prices everything, sets up the house, advertises the sale, runs the sale days, handles payment and, with a good company, clears out what's left. More: What does an estate sale company do?

What should I ask an estate sale company before hiring?

Ask if they're insured, whether you'll get a written contract, when you'll be paid, what happens to the leftovers, and what it will cost all in. More: 5 questions to ask before you hire an estate sale company

Is an estate sale or an auction better for a house full of belongings?

For a full house of furniture and household goods, an estate sale is often the better fit, because buyers walk through the home and pay tagged prices for everyday items. An auction can do better for a few high-value pieces or a collection with a national following. More: Estate sale, auction or cleanout? What fits an Erie County house

When does a cleanout make more sense than an estate sale?

When little of value is left in the house, or when there is no time for a sale. A cleanout empties the house quickly, but you pay a hauling fee and get nothing back for the contents. More: Estate sale, auction or cleanout? What fits an Erie County house

Why not just run the sale myself?

You can, but pricing, advertising, setup, staffing and cleanup take far more time than most people expect. I handle all of it, and careful pricing and promotion help the sale bring in more. More: Estate sale vs. auction vs. cleanout

Do you buy estates or items outright?

No. I run the sale for you, so the money from what sells goes to you instead of to a buyer's profit.

Are you BBB accredited?

Yes. Better Selling With Bailey is a BBB Accredited Business with the Better Business Bureau of Western Pennsylvania.

Is there a minimum sale size?

No. For smaller estates I'll walk the client through the numbers first, since the $995 flat fee is a bigger share of a small sale. More: An estate sale partner for Erie County realtors, attorneys and senior communities

Is my house worth a full sale?

Maybe not, and I'll tell you at the free walkthrough. The flat fee is a bigger share of a small sale, so I'll give you an honest estimate before you sign anything. More: Downsizing sales in Erie County

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. I take on only 2 to 3 sales a month, and spring and summer dates go first. More: Downsizing sales in Erie County

Before the sale

What should I do first after a parent passes?

Start with the free first 30 days checklist. It covers what to do first, what to keep, and what can wait. More: 3 mistakes families make clearing out a parent's house

Should I throw things away before an estate sale?

No. Wait until someone who knows what sells has walked through the house. Valuables and collectibles often hide in places that look like junk. More: 3 mistakes families make clearing out a parent's house

What papers should I keep when clearing out a parent's house?

Keep every paper until the executor has gone through it, especially the will, deeds, vehicle titles, insurance policies, bank and retirement statements, and recent tax returns. More: What not to throw away before an estate sale

Can I remove certain items before the sale?

Absolutely. Any items you'd like to keep, gift to family, or handle separately can be set aside before we begin pricing and staging. Just let us know in advance so we can plan the sale around what's actually available to sell. More: How We Price Items at Better Selling With Bailey

Should I sell the valuable items before the estate sale?

Usually not. The best pieces are what bring buyers to the sale, and once they're there, they buy everything else too. More: What actually sells at an estate sale, ranked

Should I get an appraisal before an estate sale?

For most household items and typical collectibles, our research process is sufficient and a separate appraisal isn't necessary. For exceptionally rare or highly specialized items, we may recommend consulting a specialist ourselves, so you generally don't need to arrange this on your own beforehand. More: How We Price Items at Better Selling With Bailey

What if I'm not sure what to keep?

Set those things aside or mark them, and we'll talk them through at the walkthrough. Anything you decide to keep stays off the sale. More: Downsizing sales in Erie County

Should I keep the utilities on in an empty house?

Yes, especially in winter. An empty house in Erie can freeze its pipes. Keep the heat on and tell your insurance company the house is empty. More: 3 mistakes families make clearing out a parent's house

Pricing and sale days

How do you price items?

With current market data, recent sold listings (not asking prices) and years of buying and selling experience. Read more in how I price items. More: How I price estate sale items

What kinds of items sell best?

Tools, collectibles, LEGO, Star Wars, sports and trading cards, video games, hunting and fishing gear, Harley-Davidson merchandise, vintage clothing, antiques and household goods all do well. More: What sells at an estate sale

What doesn't sell at estate sales?

Tube TVs, encyclopedias, big TV cabinets and pianos are the hardest to sell. Plan for them up front: at my sales, leftovers are kept, donated or removed free, and large furniture removal is an optional $295. More: What actually sells at an estate sale, ranked

Do I need to be there during the sale?

No. Most clients aren't. I handle setup, pricing, customers, checkout and cleanup.

What if we live out of town?

That's common. With a managed estate sale you don't need to be there for setup or the sale. I pay clients as soon as the sale ends. More: How to clear out a parent's house in Erie County

Is the sale held inside or outside?

Usually inside the home, which works year-round. A garage or yard sale is an option too, especially if you're still living in the house.

What if it rains?

Weather only matters for outdoor sales. If severe weather forces a cancellation before the sale starts, you get a 90% refund of fees paid, or you can put them toward a new date. The other 10% covers planning and advertising already done. Whenever possible, I'll reschedule rather than cancel.

What happens to things that don't sell?

You decide. Keep them, donate them, or have them removed at no charge. Large furniture is the one exception: hauling it away is an optional $295, and you don't have to decide until the sale ends. More: Estate cleanouts in Erie County

How long does it take to clear out a house?

It depends on the size of the house and how much is in it. Plan backward from your deadline, and book an estate sale company early, since good dates fill up. More: How to clear out a parent's house in Erie County

For executors

Can an executor hold an estate sale before the estate is settled?

Often, but ask the estate attorney first. Set aside anything the will gives to a specific person, let the family choose keepsakes, and keep a written record of what was sold. More: An executor's guide to the house contents in Pennsylvania

Do household contents count for Pennsylvania inheritance tax?

Yes. Pennsylvania taxes the tangible personal property of a resident who has died, including furniture, antiques and jewelry, at its fair market value on the date of death. More: An executor's guide to the house contents in Pennsylvania

When is the Pennsylvania inheritance tax return due?

Within nine months of the death. Paying within three months earns a 5% discount. Your estate attorney or accountant can confirm the details for your estate. More: An executor's guide to the house contents in Pennsylvania

How does an estate sale help with the estate's records?

With Better Selling With Bailey you're paid the day the sale ends, with a written statement of what sold and what was deducted, which gives the executor a clear record for the estate. More: An executor's guide to the house contents in Pennsylvania
