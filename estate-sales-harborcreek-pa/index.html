Close to home

Harborcreek sits between Erie and North East, so a walkthrough here is easy to fit in, evenings and Saturdays included. Two of my recent sales were just up the road in North East: a Star Wars collector sale in May and a three-day whole-home sale in August.

The families I help here are usually clearing a parent's house, downsizing after decades in the same place, or selling a collection that's taken over the garage.

What sells from Harborcreek homes

Homes near the lake and out toward the vineyards often come with boats, fishing gear, tools and outdoor equipment on top of the household goods. My July sale in Erie was three days of hunting, fishing and outdoor gear, with brands like KUIU, Klim, Shimano and Polaris, so I know who buys it and how to reach them.

Easy to find, easy to park

A lot of Harborcreek homes are on or just off Route 5 and Buffalo Road, which makes a sale easy to find. We'll talk through parking at the walkthrough so shoppers aren't blocking the road or the neighbors.

How a Harborcreek sale works
