Estate sale, auction or cleanout? What fits an Erie County house

Three ways to empty a house, and how to tell which one fits yours.

← All posts
By Dorian BaileyPublished November 14, 2026About 4 minutes to read

Short answer: if the house is full of furniture, household goods and collectibles, an estate sale usually leaves the family the most money. An auction fits a few high-value pieces, or a collection that draws serious bidders. A cleanout fits when little of value is left, or when there's no time at all.

When an estate sale fits

An estate sale works best when the house is mostly full. Furniture, tools, kitchenware, décor and collectibles sell well when buyers can walk through the rooms and see them in person, and local collectors come out for the right sale.

It also fits when the house has to be empty by a date, like a closing or a listing. I plan every sale backward from that date. With me, the cost is a flat $995 plus 25% of what sells, so on a $10,000 sale the family is paid $7,005 the day the sale ends. Here's how that compares with other Erie companies.

When an auction fits

An auction makes sense for a few pieces worth a lot, or for a specialized collection with buyers all over the country. Bidders compete, and the price can climb past what a tag would ask.

Before you sign, ask three things: the commission, the buyer's premium buyers will pay, and what happens to items that don't sell or are worth too little to list.

When a cleanout fits

A cleanout is the fastest way to empty a house. It fits when little of value is left, when time is very short, or after a sale to clear what didn't sell. You pay a hauling fee, usually by volume, and nothing comes back to you for the contents.

You can use more than one

Many houses need two: a sale for most of the contents, then a cleanout for the rest. With me, the cleanout part is included. Leftovers are kept, donated or removed free, and hauling away large furniture is optional at $95 per item (never more than $700 total). Here's how the cleanout works.

Common questions

Is an estate sale or an auction better for a house full of belongings?

For a full house of furniture and household goods, an estate sale is often the better fit, because buyers walk through the home and pay tagged prices for everyday items. An auction can do better for a few high-value pieces or a collection with a national following.

How much does an estate sale cost in Erie, PA?

Most estate sale companies in Erie charge a commission of 35% to 50% of total sales. Better Selling With Bailey charges a flat $995 plus 25% of sales, and the family is paid the day the sale ends.

What happens to items that don't sell at an estate sale?

With Better Selling With Bailey, leftovers are kept, donated or removed free, your choice. Hauling away large furniture is optional at $95 per item (never more than $700 total), decided after the sale.

When does a cleanout make more sense than an estate sale?

When little of value is left in the house, or when there is no time for a sale. A cleanout empties the house quickly, but you pay a hauling fee and get nothing back for the contents.
