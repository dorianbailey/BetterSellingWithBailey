How to shop an estate sale like a pro: 5 tips

Five simple habits that help you find the good stuff, plus how my sales work in Erie County.

← All posts
By Dorian BaileyPublished November 11, 2026About 3 minutes to read

Estate sales are some of the best places to find tools, collectibles, furniture and vintage pieces for a fraction of retail. The shoppers who do best aren't lucky. They're prepared. Here are five habits worth building.

1. Get the photos first

Most sales post preview photos before they open. Look them over so you know what you're hunting for. My sale alerts send the dates and preview photos as soon as a sale is announced, and the address two days before it starts.

2. Come early on day one

The standout pieces go fast. At my sales, entry is by sign-up sheet: add your name when you arrive, and you'll be let in in the order you signed.

3. Bring a tape measure

Know your space before you fall for that dresser. Measure doorways and the spot it's going, and keep the numbers on your phone.

4. Plan your ride home

Bring a truck, or a friend, for the big stuff. At my sales, picking up and hauling large items is up to the buyer, though depending on the item my helpers can help you move it.

5. Check every room

Garages, basements and closets hide great finds. Don't stop at the living room.

How payment and discounts work at my sales

  • Payment: cash is preferred. Venmo and cards are accepted too, with a processing fee added.
  • Holds: I can hold an item once it's paid for in full.
  • Last day: everything is 50% off.
  • Last hour: everything left is free, except high-value items.

Early on the first day if you want the best pieces. At my sales, entry is by sign-up sheet, in the order people sign.

Do estate sales take credit cards?

Many do. At my sales, cash is preferred, and Venmo and cards are accepted with a processing fee added.

When are estate sales cheapest?

Usually on the last day. At my sales, everything is 50% off on the last day, and in the last hour everything left is free, except high-value items.
