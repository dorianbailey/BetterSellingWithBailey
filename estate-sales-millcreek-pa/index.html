Selling a family home in Millcreek

Millcreek is Erie's largest suburb, and many of its houses have been in the same family for decades. That usually means a full basement, a garage and closets nobody has opened in years. The house holds far more than anyone expects, and most of it can be sold. I sort it, price it, sell it and clear what's left.

Millcreek also borders the city, Fairview and Summit Township, so shoppers can reach a sale in minutes. That helps a sale draw a crowd on the first morning, when the best pieces go.

Signs, parking and neighbors

Millcreek limits signs placed away from the property, so I don't lean on roadside signs. Sales are advertised online and to my sale-alert list, and the address goes out two days before the sale. If your street is narrow, or your neighborhood has an HOA or deed rules, tell me at the walkthrough and we'll plan dates and parking around them.

Moving a parent into senior living?

Millcreek has several senior communities, including Manchester Commons on West Lake Road, Saint Mary's at Asbury Ridge on West Ridge Road and LECOM's Parkside at Westminster. If a move-in date is already set, I'll plan the sale backward from it so the house is cleared in time. Senior communities can also refer families to me directly.

How a Millcreek sale works

We start with a free walkthrough and an honest estimate. I price every item from what similar items have actually sold for, set up and advertise the sale, and run it with my team. You're paid the day it ends, and leftovers are kept, donated or removed free. It's a flat $995 plus 25% of total sales. Can you hold a sale in a neighborhood with an HOA?
