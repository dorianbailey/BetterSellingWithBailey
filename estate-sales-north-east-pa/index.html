Why work with someone local

I grew up in North East and graduated from North East High School, so I'm close by for the walkthrough, setup and every hour of the sale. A North East sale draws neighbors, and I advertise on multiple platforms so collectors and buyers from Erie and beyond hear about it too.

How it works

  1. Free walkthrough. We walk through the house together, and I give you honest expectations before you sign anything.
  2. Plan and contract. We pick sale dates and put the agreement in writing.
  3. Pricing and setup. I research and price every item using recent sold listings, then set everything out so it's easy to shop.
  4. Advertising. I promote the sale on multiple platforms so the right buyers show up.
  5. Sale days. My team and I handle customers and checkout. You don't need to be there.
  6. Paid and cleared. You're paid as soon as the sale ends. Leftovers are kept, donated or removed free; hauling away large furniture is an optional $295 you can decide on at the end.

What it costs

A flat $995 plus 25% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. A $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee; everything else comes out of the sale proceeds. On a $10,000 sale, for example, you're paid $7,005 the day the sale ends, after the $500 you paid at booking. See everything that's included, or read what estate sales cost in Erie.

Questions

Do you only work in North East?

No. North East is home base, but I run sales across Erie County, including Erie, Millcreek, Harborcreek, Fairview, Girard, Edinboro and Corry.

Can family set aside keepsakes first?

Yes. Set aside anything you're keeping or giving to family before I start pricing, and I'll plan the sale around what's actually for sale.

What happens to what doesn't sell?

You decide: keep it, donate it, or have it removed at no charge. Hauling away large furniture is an optional $295, and you don't have to decide until the sale ends.
