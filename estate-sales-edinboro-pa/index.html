A college town and a lake town

Edinboro is a mix of longtime family homes, houses near the PennWest Edinboro campus and cottages on Edinboro Lake. I help families settling a parent's estate, owners downsizing from a house or a cottage, and anyone selling a collection.

Planning around the seasons

Sales are held inside the home, so they can run any time of year. A lake cottage that's closed up for winter may sell better in spring, and we'll weigh that together at the walkthrough. In town, we can pick dates that steer clear of busy campus weekends.

Moving a parent into senior living?

Edinboro options include Edinboro Manor on Waterford Street and Edinlake Apartments on Walker Drive. If a move-in date is already set, I'll plan the sale backward from it so the house is cleared in time.

How a Edinboro sale works

We start with a free walkthrough and an honest estimate. I price every item from what similar items have actually sold for, set up and advertise the sale, and run it with my team. You're paid the day it ends, and leftovers are kept, donated or removed free. It's a flat $995 plus 25% of total sales. Edinboro questions

Can you hold a sale at a lake cottage?

Yes. A sale can be held right at the cottage, inside or outside depending on the season, and I clear what's left afterward.

Can a sale run in winter?

Yes. Sales are held inside the home, so they run year-round. For a seasonal cottage, we'll decide together whether it's worth waiting for spring.

Do you cover McKean and Waterford?

Yes. I cover all of Erie County, including McKean, Waterford and the townships between Edinboro and Erie.
