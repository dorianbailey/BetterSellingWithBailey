Downsizing

Downsizing sales in Erie County

Moving to a smaller home, an apartment or a senior community? Set aside what you're keeping, and I'll price, sell and clear the rest on your schedule.

Book a free walkthrough Call (814) 527-1079

Start with what's coming with you

The hardest part of downsizing is deciding what fits in the new place. Set aside what's moving with you and what's going to family, and mark anything you're unsure about. I plan the sale around what's actually for sale, so nothing you want to keep ends up with a price tag on it.

Built around your move date

Most downsizing sales have a date to work toward: a closing, the end of a lease or a move-in day. We pick sale dates backward from it so the house is cleared in time, and I keep you updated along the way.

As involved as you want to be

Some people want to talk through prices on the pieces that matter to them. Others would rather not be at the sale at all. Either works. Every item is priced from what similar items have actually sold for, and you're paid the day the sale ends with a written statement of what sold.

Inside the home, or in the garage or yard

Most sales are held inside the home, so they run year-round. If it makes more sense for the house, the sale can be in the garage or yard instead. The fee is the same either way.

Moving into senior living

Senior communities usually set a move-in date weeks ahead. Tell me the date at the walkthrough, and I'll plan the sale and the cleanout around it. Senior communities can also refer families to me directly.

What it costs

A flat $995 plus 25% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. A $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee; everything else comes out of the sale proceeds. On a $10,000 sale, for example, you're paid $7,005 the day the sale ends, after the $500 you paid at booking. See everything that's included.

Where I work

All of Erie County, including Erie, Millcreek, Harborcreek, North East, Fairview, Girard, Edinboro and Corry.

Questions

What if I'm not sure what to keep?

Set those things aside or mark them, and we'll talk them through at the walkthrough. Anything you decide to keep stays off the sale.

Is my house worth a full sale?

Maybe not, and I'll tell you at the free walkthrough. The flat fee is a bigger share of a small sale, so I'll give you an honest estimate before you sign anything.

What happens to what doesn't sell?

You decide: keep it, donate it, or have it removed at no charge. Hauling away large furniture is an optional $295, and you don't have to decide until the sale ends.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. I take on only 2 to 3 sales a month, and spring and summer dates go first.
