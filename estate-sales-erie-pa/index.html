Who I help in Erie

Most of my Erie clients are clearing a parent's house before it goes on the market, moving somewhere smaller, or selling a collection that has outgrown the basement. A lot of homes around Erie were built in the 1950s and '60s, and after decades in the same house the garage, basement and attic hold far more than anyone expects. I sort, price and sell it, then clear out what's left.

A recent Erie sale

In July 2026 I ran a three-day hunting, fishing, Harley-Davidson and outdoor sports sale in Erie. It had hundreds of items from brands like KUIU, Klim, Shimano, Patagonia, Polaris and Ski-Doo, laid out by category so shoppers could find what they came for.

How an Erie estate sale works

  1. Free walkthrough. We walk through the house together, and I give you honest expectations before you sign anything.
  2. Plan and contract. We pick sale dates and put the agreement in writing.
  3. Pricing and setup. I research and price every item using recent sold listings, then set everything out so it's easy to shop.
  4. Advertising. I promote the sale on multiple platforms so the right buyers show up.
  5. Sale days. My team and I handle customers and checkout. You don't need to be there.
  6. Paid and cleared. You're paid as soon as the sale ends. Leftovers are kept, donated or removed free; hauling away large furniture is an optional $295 you can decide on at the end.

What it costs

A flat $995 plus 25% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. A $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee; everything else comes out of the sale proceeds.
