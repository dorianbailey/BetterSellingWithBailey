Cleanouts

Estate cleanouts in Erie County

Every estate sale I run in Erie County ends with a full cleanout. What doesn't sell is kept, donated or removed free, so the house is ready for a listing, a closing or a move-out.

Book a free walkthrough Call (814) 527-1079

Sell first, then clear

A straight cleanout hauls away things that could have paid for it. I sell first, so buyers carry out as much as possible and you're paid for it. Then I clear what's left.

You choose what happens to leftovers

  • Keep it. Anything set aside for family stays put.
  • Donate it. Leftovers can go to donation instead of the curb.
  • Have it removed. At no charge.

You decide once the sale is over and you can see what's left.

Large furniture: an optional $295

Hauling away large furniture that didn't sell is an optional $295. Everything else is removed free. You don't have to decide until the sale ends.

Cleared by your date

Realtors, executors and families usually have a date the house has to be empty. Tell me that date at the walkthrough, and I'll plan the sale and the cleanout to meet it.

What it costs

A flat $995 plus 25% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. A $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee; everything else comes out of the sale proceeds. On a $10,000 sale, for example, you're paid $7,005 the day the sale ends, after the $500 you paid at booking. See everything that's included.

Where I work

All of Erie County, including Erie, Millcreek, Harborcreek, North East, Fairview, Girard, Edinboro and Corry.

Questions

Is the cleanout extra?

No. Removing leftovers is included with every sale. The only extra is the optional $295 for hauling away large furniture.

Can family still take things after the sale?

Yes. Anything you want kept is set aside, and you decide on leftovers once you can see what's left.

What if I only need a cleanout?

Call and tell me about the house. Most houses have more worth selling than families expect, and the free walkthrough will tell you whether a sale makes sense.
