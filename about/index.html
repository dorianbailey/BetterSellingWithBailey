About Better Selling With Bailey

An owner-run estate sale company in North East, PA, serving all of Erie County.

Better Selling With Bailey is an owner-run estate sale company based in North East, Pennsylvania, serving all of Erie County. It's run by me, Dorian Bailey. I price every item, run the sale with my helpers, clear the house, and pay the family the day the sale ends.

The short version

  • What I do: estate sales, downsizing sales, moving sales, collection sales and estate cleanouts.
  • Where: Erie County, PA, including Erie, Millcreek, Harborcreek, North East, Fairview, Girard, Edinboro and Corry.
  • Price: a flat $995 plus 25% of total sales. The $500 deposit that holds your dates counts toward it. How that compares in Erie.
  • When you're paid: the day the sale ends, with a written statement. What the statement shows.
  • Leftovers: kept, donated or removed free. Hauling away large furniture is an optional $295.
  • Minimum sale size: none. If a managed sale won't leave you enough, I'll tell you at the walkthrough.
  • Capacity: 2 to 3 sales a month, so each one gets my full attention.
  • Accreditation: BBB Accredited Business.
  • Reviews: five-star reviews on Google.
  • Contact: Call (814) 527-1079 or email bettersellingwithbailey@gmail.com.

Who I am

I grew up in North East, graduated from North East High School, and study Marketing and Management Information Systems at Penn State Behrend. Long before this business existed, I spent years buying, selling, researching and pricing items of all kinds.

Better Selling With Bailey grew out of that, with one rule: you work directly with me, from the first walkthrough to the last day of the sale.

How I work

My helpers and I run every sale together. Some clients want a say in every price; others would rather hand it all to me. Either way works. What you can count on is clear communication, a written contract before anything happens, and honest guidance the whole way through.

I don't buy estates or items outright. I run the sale for you, so the money from what sells goes to you.
