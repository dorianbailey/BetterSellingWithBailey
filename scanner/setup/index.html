Get the Price Scanner on your phone

Point your phone's camera at the code, tap the link, then add it to your home screen. Takes a minute, and you only do it once.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button ⬆. On newer iPhones, tap ••• first.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Price Scanner from your home screen and sign in there. Tap Allow when it asks for the camera.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap Add to home screen when the scanner offers it, or tap ⋮ then Add to Home screen / Install app.
  2. Open Price Scanner from your home screen and sign in.

Signing inUse the team password (ask Dorian) and your first name. Your scans and hours are logged under your name.

Clock in, then Start

Tap Clock in when you get to the sale, then Start scanner. Clock out when you leave.

Point and hold steady

Hold still on one item for a second. The price pops up and the phone says it out loud.

Not sure? Set it aside

Tap the price, then Set aside for Dorian. His price shows up on your phone.
