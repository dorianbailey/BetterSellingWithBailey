What I sell

LEGO

Sealed and retired sets, and complete builds with boxes and instructions. Whether a set is retired, complete and boxed makes a big difference.

Star Wars and vintage toys

Action figures, vehicles and books. Maker, condition and original packaging decide most of the price.

Sports and trading cards

Condition, grading, print rarity and player popularity drive value, and prices can shift week to week.

Video games and electronics

Consoles, cartridges and accessories. Completeness and working condition matter most.

Harley-Davidson

Leather jackets, apparel, signs and riding gear.

Hunting, fishing and outdoor gear

Packs, boots, calls, rods and accessories, from brands like KUIU, Klim and Shimano.

Tools and equipment

Hand and power tools, shop equipment and garage gear, which have steady local demand.

Something else?

Coins, records, comics, advertising signs and more. If people collect it, ask me.

How I price a collection

Anyone can list an item online for any price, so asking prices tell you very little. I look at what similar items have actually sold for, in similar condition, across multiple marketplaces, and I look for a pattern rather than one lucky sale. Then I price each piece to sell during the sale, not to sit. If a piece is rare enough to need a specialist's opinion, I'll tell you before the sale rather than guess. More on this in how I price items.

A collection sale, start to finish

In May 2026 I sold a longtime collector's Star Wars collection in North East: action figures, books and sealed LEGO sets, set out on shelves and tables so collectors could browse. Questions about selling a collection

Should I sell the best pieces on my own first?

Talk to me first. The standout pieces are what bring collectors to a sale, and they help everything else sell.

Can I keep some of the collection?

Yes. Set aside anything you're keeping before I start pricing, and the sale is built around what's left.

Is there a minimum size?

No. The price is the same $995 plus 25% of total sales. Because the $995 is flat, it's a bigger share of a small sale, so I'll walk you through the numbers first.
