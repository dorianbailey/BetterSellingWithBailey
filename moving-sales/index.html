Moving

Moving sales in Erie County

Moving out of Erie County for work, family or retirement? I'll run a moving sale for what you aren't taking and clear what's left, so the house is ready to list or hand over.

Book a free walkthrough Call (814) 527-1079

Sell it here instead of paying to move it

Moving furniture and household goods across the state or the country isn't cheap. Before you collect moving quotes, it helps to know what your things could bring here. The free walkthrough gives you an honest estimate, so you can decide what's worth taking.

Planned backward from moving day

We pick sale dates that work back from your moving day and, if you're selling the house, your listing or closing date. Everything goes in a written contract, and a $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee.

Already moved? You don't need to be here

If you've already left Erie County, the sale can still run. I handle setup, sale days and the cleanout. You're paid the day the sale ends, with a written statement of what sold and what was deducted.

Working with your realtor

A house shows better once it's cleared. With your OK, I'll keep your realtor posted on sale dates and the day the house is empty. Realtors can read how I work with their clients.

What it costs

A flat $995 plus 25% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. A $500 deposit holds your dates and counts toward the fee; everything else comes out of the sale proceeds. On a $10,000 sale, for example, you're paid $7,005 the day the sale ends, after the $500 you paid at booking. See everything that's included.

Where I work

All of Erie County, including Erie, Millcreek, Harborcreek, North East, Fairview, Girard, Edinboro and Corry.

Questions

What about the things I'm taking?

Set them aside or move them out before setup. I only price what's for sale.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. I take on only 2 to 3 sales a month, and spring and summer dates go first.

What happens to what doesn't sell?

You decide: keep it, donate it, or have it removed at no charge. Hauling away large furniture is an optional $295, and you don't have to decide until the sale ends.
