How to shop a Bailey sale

Sales are open to the public. Here's how entry, payment and pickup work, and when prices drop.

Get sale alerts See upcoming sales

The rules, in one place

  • The address comes out 2 days before

    Each sale is announced with the dates and preview photos. The address is released two days before the sale starts, in the listing and in sale alerts.

  • Entry is by sign-up sheet

    Add your name to the sheet when you arrive, and you'll be let in in the order you signed.

  • Cash is preferred

    Venmo and cards are accepted too, with a processing fee added.

  • Holds only when paid in full

    I can hold an item for you once it's paid for in full, not before.

  • You handle pickup of big items

    Bring a vehicle that fits and someone to help. Depending on the item, my helpers can help you move it.

  • As-is, and all sales final

    Look things over before you buy. Please park legally and keep neighbors' driveways clear.

Questions shoppers ask

When do you post the address?

Two days before the sale starts. It goes out in the sale listing and in my sale alerts, along with preview photos.

How do I get in?

Entry is by sign-up sheet. Add your name when you arrive and you'll be let in in the order you signed.

Do you take cards or Venmo?

Yes. Cash is preferred. Venmo and cards are accepted too, with a processing fee added.

Can you hold something for me?

Only once it's paid for in full.

Will you deliver or haul big items?

No. Picking up and hauling large items is up to you, so bring a vehicle that fits and someone to help. Depending on the item, my helpers can help you move it.

When do prices drop?

On the last day of the sale, everything is 50% off. In the last hour, everything left is free, except high-value items.
