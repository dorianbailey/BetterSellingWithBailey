For professionals

An estate sale partner for Erie County realtors, attorneys and senior communities

When your client needs a house emptied before a listing, a closing or a move, I handle the sale and the cleanout, and keep everyone informed along the way.

What your client gets

  • A free walkthrough and honest expectations before anything is signed.
  • A written contract, and an insured business.
  • Payment as soon as the sale ends, with a written statement of what the sale brought in and what was deducted.
  • A cleared house. Leftovers are removed free, and large furniture can be hauled away for an optional $295, decided at the end of the sale.
  • No need to be there. Good for heirs who live out of town.
  • Sales held inside the home, so they run year-round.

For realtors

An estate listing is hard to show until the house is cleared. Tell me when the house needs to be empty and I'll plan the sale around that date. With the large furniture option, everything is gone after the sale, not just the smaller items.

For estate attorneys and executors

Executors need a clean record. Every sale runs under a written contract, the client is paid when the sale ends, and they receive a statement of what the sale brought in and what was deducted for the estate's files.

For senior living communities

New residents often have a set move-in date and a house full of belongings. I plan the sale around that date and let them choose how involved they want to be in pricing.

How a referral works

  1. Send me your client's name and number through the contact form, or call (814) 323-8239.
  2. I reach out, set up a free walkthrough, and keep you in the loop if your client would like.
  3. After the sale, the client is paid and the house is cleared for the next step.

Questions from professionals

Does my client need to be at the sale?

No. That makes it a good fit for out-of-town heirs. I handle setup, the sale and cleanup, and the client is paid as soon as the sale ends.

Is there a minimum sale size?

No. For smaller estates I'll walk the client through the numbers first, since the $995 flat fee is a bigger share of a small sale.

What does the client pay?

A flat $995 plus 20% of total sales, with free removal of leftovers. Hauling away large furniture is an optional $295 the client can add at the end of the sale.
