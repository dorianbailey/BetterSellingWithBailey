What estate sale companies in Erie charge, and what you actually keep

Typical commissions, minimums and extra fees in Erie County, with side-by-side math on what a family keeps.

← All posts
By Dorian BaileyPublished November 9, 2026About 6 minutes to read

If you're comparing estate sale companies in Erie County, the commission is the number that matters most, and it's often the one people find hardest to pin down. Here's what's typical, what extra fees to ask about, and what a family actually keeps.

The short answer

According to HomeLight's guide to Erie estate sale companies, most local companies charge 35% to 50% commission on total sales and want an estimated $5,000 to $10,000 in sales before they'll take a job. Nationally, a 2024 survey of 774 estate sale businesses by EstateSales.NET put the average commission at 40%. HomeLight, citing EstateSales.NET, says a typical estate sale brings in $18,000 to $20,000.

Extra fees to ask about

Commission isn't always the whole bill. In EstateSales.NET's 2019 survey, trash removal was the most common extra fee, charged by 75% of companies, followed by advertising (46%) and professional cleaning (29%). Consumers' Checkbook notes that minimum fees of $500 to $3,000 are also common. Before you sign, ask:

  • What is the commission, and is there a minimum fee?
  • Are advertising, cleanup and removal of leftovers included?
  • Do you charge for card processing?
  • When will I be paid, and will I get a statement?
  • Are you insured, and is everything in a written contract?

Common questions

What is a normal estate sale commission in Erie?

HomeLight reports that Erie estate sale companies typically charge 35% to 50% of total sales, with minimums of $5,000 to $10,000 in expected sales.

Do estate sale companies charge extra for cleanout?

Many do. Trash removal was the most common extra fee in EstateSales.NET's 2019 survey. Always ask what removal costs before you sign.

Is a lower commission always better?

Not always. Compare what you'd keep after every fee, and look at the company's reviews, insurance, contract and how fast they pay.
